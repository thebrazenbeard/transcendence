# Capture Layers

Status: capture-domain architecture.

Transcendence uses multiple capture layers because current science does not establish one sufficient representation of a person.

A layer's presence means "potentially relevant evidence domain," not "proven necessary state."

## 1. Structural substrate

Candidate measurements include:

- neuron identity and cell class;
- glial/support-cell classes;
- neuronal and glial morphology;
- axonal and dendritic geometry;
- synaptic connectivity and location;
- synapse type;
- myelination and conduction-relevant structure;
- vascular/metabolic context where causally relevant;
- extracellular microenvironment structure where causally relevant.

A connectome is a high-value structural representation, not a complete-person claim.

## 2. Effective connectivity and synaptic state

Connection presence alone does not establish present causal influence.

Capture candidates include:

- synaptic strength or best available proxy;
- excitatory/inhibitory/transmitter identity;
- receptor composition and density;
- short-term plasticity state;
- long-term plasticity markers;
- transmission delay and conduction properties;
- neuromodulatory sensitivity;
- dendritic location and local integration context.

`POSSIBLE_SIGNAL_PATH != CURRENT_CAUSAL_INFLUENCE`

## 3. Cellular and molecular state

Candidate variables include:

- cell type/subtype;
- transcriptomic state;
- epigenetic state;
- ion-channel expression/state;
- receptor expression;
- protein/signaling states implicated in plasticity;
- spine and dendritic microstructure;
- metabolic state;
- glial functional state;
- local neuromodulatory sensitivity.

The project does not assert that all such variables are required. Exclusion should eventually be evidence-driven rather than assumed.

## 4. Dynamic neural state

Potential fast-state variables include:

- membrane-potential/electrophysiological state;
- recent firing/activity patterns;
- oscillatory relationships and synchrony;
- transient ensembles/coalitions;
- working-memory configuration;
- neuromodulator concentrations/gradients;
- current homeostatic/interoceptive state.

Research should determine which dynamic variables regenerate from slower state and which require explicit initialization.

## 5. Whole-body regulatory and embodiment state

Human cognition is embodied.

Candidate evidence includes:

- endocrine state;
- autonomic/interoceptive state;
- chronic physiological baselines;
- sensorimotor calibration;
- body schema;
- persistent pain/fatigue states where cognitively material;
- sensory limitations and learned compensation.

A reconstruction in a different body may require translation or recalibration rather than literal reproduction.

## 6. Cognitive phenotype

Build subject-specific evidence for:

- autobiographical memory;
- semantic knowledge;
- language use;
- procedural skills;
- preferences and values;
- emotional associations;
- habits and learned strategies;
- relationships/person models;
- self-concept and identity claims;
- recurring goals/conations;
- moral/social constraints;
- personality features;
- characteristic reasoning and uncertainty behavior.

This is independent qualification material as well as reconstruction input.

## 7. Longitudinal cognitive shadow

Capture repeatedly over life rather than depending only on a terminal scan.

Potential channels:

- writing and speech;
- autobiographical interviews;
- recognition probes;
- preference/value judgments;
- perceptual tasks;
- reaction-time tasks;
- problem-solving traces;
- motor/procedural tasks;
- social decisions;
- emotional/appraisal responses;
- learning trajectories;
- corrections and changes of mind.

Longitudinal evidence helps distinguish stable traits from temporary states and gives future reconstruction tests subject-specific constraints.

## Capture quality dimensions

Each capture method should document:

- spatial resolution;
- temporal resolution;
- coverage;
- invasiveness;
- destructive/non-destructive status;
- measurement error;
- known artifacts;
- subject state during capture;
- repeatability;
- calibration method;
- raw-data availability.

## Terminal-state caution

Terminal injury, agonal physiology, anesthesia, seizure activity, preservation delay, fixation chemistry, and capture damage may produce high-resolution measurements of an abnormal state.

Such data remain valuable but must not silently overwrite ordinary-life baselines.
