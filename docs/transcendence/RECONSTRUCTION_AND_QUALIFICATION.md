# Reconstruction and Qualification

Status: reconstruction/qualification architecture.

## Reconstruction candidate

A reconstruction candidate is a reproducible instantiation generated from a named subject archive snapshot, interpretation set, translation version, target architecture, and initialization procedure.

Minimum metadata:

- subject archive ID;
- HCSA snapshot/version;
- interpretation bundle/version;
- translation model/version;
- target substrate/architecture/version;
- initialization procedure;
- embodiment assumptions;
- generated/inferred/unknown fraction by subsystem or state family;
- incompatible/unmapped source state;
- qualification-suite version;
- qualification history.

A reconstruction's self-report about being the original person is evidence about its self-model, not independent proof of continuity.

## Train/test isolation

A digital replica can appear convincing by memorizing data supplied during construction.

Transcendence therefore requires qualification evidence that is distinguishable from reconstruction inputs.

Potential holdouts include:

- private recognition probes;
- unusual semantic associations;
- episodic details;
- procedural tasks;
- perceptual discriminations;
- novel value trade-offs;
- problem-solving strategy traces;
- person-specific cue associations;
- predictions recorded before reconstruction.

Exposure lineage matters. Once a holdout is revealed to training, tuning, prompting, or repair, it no longer counts as independent evidence for that candidate.

## Qualification dimensions

### Structural fidelity

Coverage and accuracy of subject-specific structural state.

### State fidelity

Coverage and accuracy of subject-specific synaptic, molecular, regulatory, and dynamic state.

### Autobiographical fidelity

Recovery of subject-specific autobiographical content under tests not merely copied into a prompt/database response.

### Semantic fidelity

Subject-specific organization of knowledge, associations, concepts, and idiosyncratic semantic structure.

### Procedural fidelity

Retention of learned procedures/skills that cannot be demonstrated by verbal recollection alone.

### Preference/value fidelity

Stable and context-sensitive trade-offs on novel cases rather than repeated survey answers.

### Affective fidelity

Patterns of appraisal, salience, attraction, aversion, attachment, and regulation.

### Conative fidelity

Generation and arbitration of goals, priorities, conflicts, and persistence.

### Self-model fidelity

Coherent relationship to the archived person's history without coaching the candidate to assert identity.

### Social-model fidelity

Relationship-specific knowledge and expectations without cross-person leakage.

### Cognitive-style fidelity

Characteristic reasoning strategies, uncertainty behavior, error patterns, heuristics, and problem-solving trajectories on novel tasks.

## Claim ladder

### ARCHIVE_CAPTURED

A subject archive exists with quantified coverage and provenance.

### DIGITAL_REPLICA

A system reproduces substantial subject-like outputs or behavior. This is an imitation claim, not a continuity claim.

### FUNCTIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION_CANDIDATE

Broad subject-specific cognitive functions are reconstructed and independently tested.

### CAUSAL_ORGANIZATION_RECONSTRUCTION_CANDIDATE

Evidence supports preservation/reconstruction of relevant internal causal organization beyond output mimicry.

### CONTINUITY_PRESERVATION_CANDIDATE

The method deliberately preserves causal/temporal continuity and has evidence supporting that process-level claim. Subjective continuity remains unresolved.

### SUBJECTIVE_CONTINUITY

Current framework status: `UNKNOWN`.

This level is not self-awardable without a future defensible empirical criterion.

## Qualification reports

Use PASS / CONDITIONAL PASS / FAIL only against a named capability and frozen candidate.

A useful report contains:

- exact candidate/archive/translation version;
- capability tested;
- independent evidence used;
- holdout contamination status;
- observed failures;
- result;
- claim ceiling;
- remaining uncertainty.

## Anti-impersonation invariant

`IMITATION_QUALITY != RECONSTRUCTION_FIDELITY`

Natural conversation may be one test surface, but never the whole qualification regime.
